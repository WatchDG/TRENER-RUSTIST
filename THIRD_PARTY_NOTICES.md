# Third-party notices

The code of this repository is licensed under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). The data in
`datasets/` is partly derived from third-party documentation: the corpora in
`datasets/rust-docs/` contain doc comments extracted from rustdoc JSON, and the
task sets in `datasets/rust/` paraphrase and quote the same documentation. That
material remains under the licenses of its authors, listed below.

Task sets generated by the repository's own scripts (`datasets/reverse-rust/`)
contain no third-party text.

The benchmarks in `bench/` are third-party test sets converted to the task
format as is (`scripts/build_bench_gsm8k.py`).

`trener/architectures/spark2_5.py` reimplements the Spark-X2.6 architecture
after `configuration_spark.py` and `modeling_spark.py` of
[XHToken/Spark-X2.6-1.7B](https://huggingface.co/XHToken/Spark-X2.6-1.7B)
(Apache-2.0, Copyright 2026 The XHToken team and the HuggingFace Inc. team);
the Apache-2.0 text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE).

`trener/audio.py` implements the windowed-sinc resampling of
[torchaudio](https://github.com/pytorch/audio) (`torchaudio.functional.resample`, BSD-2-Clause, Copyright (c)
2017 Facebook Inc. (Soumith Chintala)): the kernel construction follows its algorithm.

Speech corpora built by `scripts/build_fleurs_speech_corpus.py` are not part of the repository; they are made from
[FLEURS](https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs) (`google/fleurs`, revision
70bb2e84b976b7e960aa89f1c648e09c59f894dd, CC-BY-4.0, Google) and keep its license. The spoken questions of
`datasets/rust-voice/` are synthesized by LFM3-Omni-2B (LFM Open License v1.0) from the questions of
`datasets/rust/rust-keywords-0001`.
